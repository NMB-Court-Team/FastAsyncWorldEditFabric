2. Statement of modifications

This port was prepared in 2026. It adapts the upstream code to Minecraft 26.2
and the Fabric loader, replaces selected platform and queue implementations
with Kotlin code, adds FastAsyncWorldEdit features such as limits, history
browsing, brushes and schematic sources, and adds a Simplified Chinese
translation layer. The upstream projects do not endorse this port.
